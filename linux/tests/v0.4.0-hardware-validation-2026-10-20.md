# v0.4.0 hardware validation (2026-10-20)

Board: M5Stamp ESP32-P4 + C6 (SDIO), P4 USB Serial/JTAG on COM10.  Only the
P4 flash was written; the C6 was not touched.

v0.4.0 is v0.3.2 with the #5 SMP work and the fixes of #41 to #44: both
CPUs run by default (0079, 0080, 0081, the boot-shim's PMA/PMP for CPU1);
hush `set -e` follows POSIX, also in NOMMU subshells; the ttyGS1 console
finishes output from the interrupt and bounds the wait when the host does
not read; a `/boot` flash commit writes only the sectors that changed and
paces its `stop_machine()` calls so Wi-Fi keeps up, and the SDIO path
wakes the transmit thread on enqueue and runs it, the receive workqueue
and the interrupt work at raised priority; and handled signals return
through the kernel's own sigreturn code.  It also carries the fixes that
were on `main` after v0.3.2: 2% of `/tmp` reserved for root, MicroPython's
48 KB stack, the per-key `config.txt` apply and the NOMMU pool stats of
#39/#40, and the loopback at boot.  The checks of each change on local
builds are in `issue5-smp-2026-10-19.md`,
`issue39-40-hardware-checks-2026-10-19.md` and `issue41-44-2026-10-20.md`.

  the 2% reserve, checked in `v0.3.2-followup-checks-2026-10-19.md`.

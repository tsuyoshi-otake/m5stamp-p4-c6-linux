P4 flash was written; the C6 was not touched.  Recovery point: the full

## Not covered

- Edits from a real Windows/macOS host over USB-A.  The host write was
  emulated by a loop mount of `/tmp/boot.img` on the board, so the USB
  transport and a host's write pattern are untested on this image.
- Power loss during a kernel flash write.
- A soak of hours on this image, and a client joining the SoftAP.
